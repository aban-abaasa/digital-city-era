### Platform Overview: SupermarketEra

**SupermarketEra** is a cutting-edge retail management platform that transforms traditional supermarket operations through digital innovation, featuring:

- **Multi-Store Management** - Centralized control for supermarket chains
- **Supplier Marketplace** - B2B ordering and inventory management
- **Manager Portal** - Store operations and inventory control
- **Cashier POS System** - Point-of-sale with receipt generation
- **Customer Dashboard** - Online shopping and order tracking
- **Delivery Integration** - Bodagoera transport booking
- **Wholesale Business Mode** - Global wholesaler discovery and B2B commerce
- **PWA Support** - Installable mobile-ready application
